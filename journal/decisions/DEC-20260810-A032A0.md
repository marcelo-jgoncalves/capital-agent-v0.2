## Why this could fail

- The Selic/Tesouro Selic benchmark figure could be stale or wrong (mitigated:
  cross-checked across 3 independent sources; still worth a real-time check at
  execution time, since rates move).
- The inference about the human owner's skill base could be wrong or the two
  projects could be unrelated; if so, the digital-product recommendation below
  loses its main justification and should be revisited.
- BRL 1,000 is small enough that almost any strategy has a low absolute payoff
  in the near term; the real test is whether the chosen direction has a path
  to scale, not whether it turns BRL 1,000 into a large sum quickly.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Doing nothing** (leave BRL 1,000 idle, uninvested): baseline. Costs the
   full opportunity cost of the risk-free rate (~14% a.a. foregone) for zero
   effort and zero risk. Rejected as a permanent state, but is the correct
   default *while* nothing else has cleared the bar — money should not sit in
   a zero-yield account by default when a near-risk-free 14% a.a. alternative
   exists.
2. **B3 stock/ETF picking as primary strategy**: rejected as a primary
   strategy at this capital scale. With `max_single_live_allocation_brl` =
   BRL 100 and typical B3 brokerage/custody costs and bid-ask spreads,
   a single position this small has transaction costs that are a
   material percentage of the position itself, and BRL 500 minimum reserve
   leaves at most a handful of BRL-100 positions possible — real
   diversification is not achievable. This is a structural, capital-scale
   argument, independent of any specific stock's merit, and it is consistent
   with the human owner's own stated direction that this project should not
   focus exclusively on the stock market. Individual equities/ETFs are not
   permanently excluded — they become viable again once equity has grown
   enough that BRL 100 stops being the binding constraint — but they are not
   this cycle's priority.
3. **Crypto trading**: same capital-inefficiency problem as (2), plus higher
   volatility relative to the `hard_drawdown_freeze_pct` (20%) limit. Rejected
   as a primary strategy for the same structural reason, not because crypto is
   categorically worse.
4. **Tesouro Selic as reserve parking** (not a growth thesis, a cash-management
   decision): comparing "BRL 1,000 idle" vs. "BRL 1,000 in Tesouro Selic," the
   latter dominates — near-zero risk, near-zero effort, ~14% a.a. instead of
   ~0%. This does not compete with a growth strategy; it is what the reserve
   portion (BRL 500 minimum under policy) should be doing while other
   experiments are researched, once an account exists to hold it (see
   Decision below — blocked on Gate H1).
5. **Digital product / service leveraging existing skill and audience**
   (micro-SaaS, productized consulting offer, paid content, lead generation
   tied to the existing AI/AWS/DevOps consulting positioning): not capital
   constrained in the same way — the binding constraint is time/effort and
   idea validation, not the BRL 100 allocation cap. Plausibly the
   highest-expected-value category at this capital scale precisely because it
   does not compete for the scarce BRL 100 slots. This is the recommended
   focus for the next concrete experiment (see companion experiment record).

## Expected-value view

- Downside: BRL 0 committed by this decision itself (it is a direction-setting
  decision, not a spend).
- Base case: reserve capital begins earning ~14% a.a. instead of ~0% once a
  Tesouro Direto/brokerage account exists; a small, cheap validation
  experiment is designed and run on the digital-offer thesis.
- Upside: the digital-offer thesis validates and becomes the primary growth
  vector, decoupled from the BRL 100/position ceiling that caps market-based
  strategies at this equity level.
- Confidence: medium. High confidence in the capital-scale math against
  stock-picking; medium confidence in the digital-offer inference, which
  depends on an assumption about the human owner's skill base stated above.
