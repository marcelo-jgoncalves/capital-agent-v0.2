# Decision: Pivot away from personally-delivered consulting — minimize human labor, not just human financial exposure

- Date/time: 2026-08-10T12:20:00-03:00
- Decision ID: DEC-20260810-875930
- Category: strategy (supersedes the business-model portion of `DEC-20260810-A032A0`)

- Current experiment equity: BRL 1,000.00
- Maximum plausible loss: N/A at this stage
- Time to first useful feedback: immediate (direction correction)

## Opportunity

Not a new opportunity — a correction to the mandate itself, from the human
owner directly: "a ideia era eu ter que intervir o mínimo possível... você é
o responsável por esse experimento e pela busca de resultado." Minimal human
intervention was already the design goal (`AI_OPERATING_MANUAL.md` "Minimize
*unnecessary* human intervention"), but this session's first concrete
proposal — a personally-delivered consulting micro-offer
(`EXP-20260810-1F7147`, now killed) — violated it in a way not caught until
the human pointed it out: delivering consulting requires the human's
ongoing personal time and expertise applied per client, which is a much
larger and more open-ended form of intervention than the custody-invariant
minimum (financial execution, critical-decision approval).

## Mechanism of return

Correcting the constraint: any candidate opportunity from here on must not
require the human's ongoing personal labor to deliver value to a customer.
The custody invariant already means the human is the irreducible minimum for
moving money and authorizing critical decisions — that floor cannot be
lowered, but it should not be built on top of either.

## Evidence

- Human owner's own words, this session: "A ideia era eu ter que intervir o
  mínimo possível no processo de multiplicar esse dinheiro. De modo que
  consultoria já envolveria muita intervenção minha... Você é o responsável
  por esse experimento e pela busca de resultado."
- `AI_OPERATING_MANUAL.md`'s responsibility split already stated this in
  principle ("Capital Agent: THINKS, RESEARCHES, DECIDES... RECOMMENDS."
  / "Human Owner: CUSTODIES AND MOVES REAL MONEY AND AUTHORIZES CRITICAL
  DECISIONS.") — the consulting proposal was inconsistent with that split
  even though it passed every other check (policy, criticality,
  capital-efficiency) in `DEC-20260810-A032A0`. That decision's process was
  sound; its business-model instantiation was not re-checked against this
  specific constraint. Recorded as a lesson, not just fixed silently.

## Why this could fail

- Any category of business that produces revenue without ongoing human labor
  usually requires more upfront building effort (software, automation,
  content) instead — trading "intervention over time" for "effort now." The
  effort now still has to come from somewhere; this decision assigns it to
  the Capital Agent's own build capability (I can write and ship code),
  not to the human's calendar.
- Fully labor-free revenue is rare; "minimal," not "zero," human involvement
  is the realistic target — account creation (Gate H2), payment setup, and
  occasional low-volume support are still expected, and are consistent with
  the custody-invariant floor, not an expansion of it.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Keep the consulting direction, just automate scheduling/intake**: still
   requires the human to actually perform each engagement. Rejected — the
   binding constraint (personal time) isn't automated away by better
   scheduling.
2. **Do nothing / only pursue Tesouro Selic**: consistent with minimal labor,
   but gives up on the higher-expected-value, capital-efficient growth
   vector entirely. Rejected as the sole path — reserve management continues
   in parallel (`APR-20260810-3DA546`, still pending), this decision is about
   the *growth* vector specifically.
3. **AI-built, low-maintenance digital product** (software, automation,
   template/starter-kit, content system): the human's labor is front-loaded
   into account/credential creation and periodic approval, not ongoing
   delivery. This is what `AI_OPERATING_MANUAL.md`'s business-model list
   already covers (software, micro-SaaS, digital products, licensing) and
   fits the corrected constraint. **Selected.**

## Concrete next experiment (replacing the killed one)

Grounded in evidence already available in this session, not invented: the
human owner has a real, production-grade AWS serverless engineering system
(`marcelo-goncalves-blog`, described in its own README as an
"AWS Serverless Editorial Platform" with Terraform-managed infrastructure,
CI/CD, Lambda/DynamoDB/CloudFront architecture, and documented engineering
standards). Packaging validated pieces of that system — e.g. Terraform
modules, a CI/CD pipeline pattern, an architecture reference — into a
one-time-purchase digital product (a "starter kit" / template sold on a
marketplace such as Gumroad) is:

- **Low incremental build effort**: extraction, generalization (removing
  anything specific/private) and documentation, which the Capital Agent can
  do largely autonomously as an engineering task, not a new invention.
- **Low ongoing human labor**: a digital download, not a service — support
  volume at this scale is expected to be low and episodic, not per-customer
  delivery time.
- **Grounded in already-proven, working technical assets**, not a
  speculative new build.

This is a *proposal to research and prepare*, not a proposal to publish.
Publishing anything under the human owner's name/brand is unconditionally a
critical decision (`CRITICAL_DECISIONS.md`, "publishing or communicating
publicly in the human owner's name") — research, extraction and drafting can
proceed without a gate; going live requires a separate critical-decision
approval package before anything is made public, including a check that
nothing private, client-confidential, or credential-bearing is exposed.

## Expected-value view

- Downside: near-zero capital (marketplace listing is typically free or a
  small percentage-of-sale fee, no upfront cost); the human's time cost is
  limited to account creation and a go/no-go review before publishing.
- Base case: a real, sellable digital product exists, tested against genuine
  market interest via marketplace listing/discovery and possibly a small
  paid-traffic test (reusing the same non-critical-threshold pattern as the
  killed experiment, but pointed at a product page, not a service booking).
- Upside: a second, larger asset (e.g. a broader course/template library) if
  the first product validates.
- Confidence: medium — grounded in real assets, but marketplace demand for
  developer starter-kits is genuinely uncertain and not yet researched in
  depth (next step, not yet done).

## Decision

**Observe → prepare.** Kill the consulting micro-offer
(`experiments/archive/EXP-20260810-1F7147.json`) as a mandate-fit failure,
not a market failure. Redirect the growth-vector search toward AI-built,
low-maintenance digital products. Next concrete step: scope and draft a
starter-kit/template product derived from the human owner's existing AWS
serverless engineering work, as research/preparation only — no public action
until a critical-decision approval package is prepared and authorized.

## Kill condition

Revisit this direction if research into marketplace demand for
developer-focused starter kits/templates shows the category itself is not
viable (e.g. saturated, low willingness to pay, or the extraction effort
turns out to be much larger than expected because too much is
project-specific and not generalizable).

## Review condition

Next `weekly` scheduler cycle, or sooner once initial market research on the
starter-kit category is complete.
