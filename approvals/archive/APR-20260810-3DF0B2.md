## Why this action

Tesouro Selic's real minimum purchase lot is BRL 196.64, above the current `max_single_live_allocation_pct_equity` cap of 10% (BRL 100 at current BRL 1,000 equity) — `HER-20260810-5961BE` could not be executed as a result and was cancelled. Rather than raising the general single-allocation cap (which also governs risky market positions), propose a new, separate field in `config/policy.json`: `max_single_reserve_instrument_pct_equity` = 0.3 (BRL 300 at current equity), applying only to purchases explicitly flagged as low-risk reserve instruments via a new `--reserve-instrument` flag on `request-execution` (an explicit, audited claim, not inferred from the asset name — asserting it falsely on a risky instrument is exactly the kind of thing this record makes traceable after the fact). The general `max_single_live_allocation_pct_equity` (10%) is unchanged for everything else. `min_cash_reserve_pct_equity` (50%, BRL 500) is unaffected and remains the binding floor: BRL 300 committed still leaves BRL 700 cash, above the BRL 500 minimum.

## Alternatives

- **Do nothing**: keep the BRL 100 cap as the only limit. Consequence: Tesouro Selic (and likely most Tesouro Direto instruments, which trade in similar-sized lots) becomes permanently unreachable at this equity level, closing off the specific reserve-parking action `DEC-20260810-A032A0` already recommended and the human owner already approved in principle. Rejected — the underlying reserve-parking thesis (idle cash earning ~14% instead of ~0%) doesn't stop being correct just because the first instrument tried had an inconvenient lot size.
- **Raise the general `max_single_live_allocation_pct_equity` cap instead** (e.g. to 30% across the board): simpler (one field, no new flag), but weakens the cap for genuinely risky market bets too, which is a larger and less justified relaxation than this decision needs. Rejected in favor of the narrower, differentiated cap.
- **One-off manual exception for this single purchase, no new field**: rejected — would not be auditable or repeatable, and the next Tesouro Direto purchase would hit the same wall again.

## Critic assessment

- This relaxation is scoped as narrowly as the actual problem: it does not touch the cap governing risky market positions, does not touch the minimum cash reserve, and only activates when a request explicitly self-declares `--reserve-instrument` — a claim that is now permanently recorded in the execution request and auditable.
- Residual risk: the explicit-flag design trusts the requester's honesty about what counts as a "reserve instrument." Mitigated by full auditability (the flag and its justification are recorded in `execution/human_requests/`) and by the fact that the human owner is the one who ultimately executes the trade on their own platform regardless of how the agent classified it — misclassifying something as a reserve instrument doesn't let the agent move money, it only changes what the agent is willing to *recommend*.
- 0.3 (BRL 300) as the chosen ceiling gives roughly 50% headroom above the BRL 196.64 minimum lot (covering reasonable unit-price drift between quote and purchase) without approaching the BRL 500 minimum reserve floor. This is a judgment call, not derived from a formula — the human owner may prefer a different number.
- Recommend: approve, with the number open to the human owner's adjustment.
